# Progress Review, Intervention, and Appeal

This prepared prototype document is not official university advice. It is a controlled policy-like scenario for testing Uni-Navigator retrieval, citation, and evidence checking.

Academic progression review may occur when a student fails courses, does not meet program requirements, or has repeated unsuccessful attempts. A progression notice may ask the student to complete an intervention plan, attend advising, reduce study load, or show cause why they should continue in the program.

Students should read the notice carefully because response deadlines can be short. A response may need to explain academic difficulties, personal circumstances, support already sought, and a realistic plan for future study.

If a student is considering an appeal, has visa implications, is close to exclusion, or is unsure how a progression decision affects enrolment, they should contact authorised university staff or an authorised advocacy service before the deadline.
