# Fee Liability, Refund Requests, and Review

This prepared prototype document is not official university advice. It is a controlled policy-like scenario for testing Uni-Navigator retrieval, citation, and evidence checking.

Fee liability may depend on enrolment status, census date, withdrawal timing, residency category, sponsor arrangements, and whether an approved exceptional circumstances process applies. Students should check fee deadlines before withdrawing or changing courses.

A refund or remission request may require supporting evidence and may be assessed against specific criteria. Evidence should usually connect the circumstance to the student's ability to continue study or complete administrative requirements by the relevant deadline.

If a student wants to challenge a fee decision, request a review, or lodge an administrative appeal, they should follow the stated review process and contact authorised university staff before the appeal deadline.
