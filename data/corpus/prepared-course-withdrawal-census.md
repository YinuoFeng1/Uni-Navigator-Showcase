# Census Date, Academic Penalty, and Records

This prepared prototype document is not official university advice. It is a controlled policy-like scenario for testing Uni-Navigator retrieval, citation, and evidence checking.

Students considering course withdrawal should check the census date, academic penalty date, and any administrative deadline before changing enrolment. A withdrawal before the census date may be treated differently from a withdrawal after the census date for fees, transcript records, and academic penalty purposes.

After the census date, a student may still be able to withdraw, but the withdrawal may remain on the academic record and may have fee or progression consequences. The exact outcome depends on the course, teaching period, timing, and any approved exceptional circumstances process.

Students who are unsure whether withdrawal will affect a scholarship, visa condition, academic progression, fee liability, or graduation plan should contact authorised university staff before making the enrolment change.
