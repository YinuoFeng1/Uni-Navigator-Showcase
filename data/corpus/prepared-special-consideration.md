# Eligibility and Evidence

This prepared prototype document is not official university advice. It is a controlled policy-like scenario for testing Uni-Navigator retrieval, citation, and evidence checking.

Students may request special consideration when short-term illness, accident, bereavement, or other exceptional circumstances affect an exam, placement, or assessment task. The request should explain how the circumstance affected the assessment and should normally be submitted by the stated application deadline.

Supporting evidence may include a medical certificate, practitioner statement, accident report, or another relevant document that connects the circumstance to the assessment impact. Students should avoid sharing unnecessary private details in a chatbot and should provide sensitive evidence only through authorised university channels.

If the case involves an exam, missed deadline, ongoing illness, disability adjustment, visa condition, or another high-stakes personal circumstance, the student should contact authorised university staff for case-specific advice.
