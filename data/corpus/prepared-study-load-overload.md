# Standard Load, Overload, and Approval

This prepared prototype document is not official university advice. It is a controlled policy-like scenario for testing Uni-Navigator retrieval, citation, and evidence checking.

A standard full-time study load is the normal enrolment pattern for the program and teaching period. An enrolment overload is a request to take more units than the standard load. Overload approval may depend on academic standing, previous results, course availability, timetable clashes, program rules, and the student's capacity to complete all assessment.

Students should not assume overload is automatically allowed. A request may need program approval before the enrolment deadline, and the student may be asked to explain why the additional study load is manageable.

Students with visa conditions, progression concerns, placement requirements, scholarship rules, or work-integrated learning commitments should contact authorised university staff before increasing their study load.
